# Capabilities need governance, not just good prompts

**Documented fact:** Google Cloud Agent Registry treats standalone skills as top-level resources with skill revisions, publisher identity, access policies, and lifecycle controls.

**Reasonable inference:** durable identity and revision history can make reusable capabilities easier to inspect and control.

**Direct similarity:** our work shares principles of controlled reuse, revision history, durable operational identity, and lifecycle governance. The architectures are not equivalent.

**Broader trend:** agent infrastructure is moving toward managed capability resources.

Our thesis is **probabilistic intelligence embedded inside deterministic systems**. People or models may propose interpretations and drafts. Deterministic application logic owns workflow state. Governed Signal-to-Content extends that boundary into evidence lineage, approval authority, persistent local state, publication authorization, multi-brand control surfaces, and command receipts.

It prepares evidence-backed candidates. It does not automate publication.
