# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome correction and distinguish evidence from interpretation.
- Respect privacy and never post credentials or private evidence.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementations, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate this code. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact channels. Maintainers will review reports in good faith and protect reporter privacy where practical.
